Revenue stream breakdown

Annual value by stream at the current inputs. Tags mark whether the stream is a reduction to the customer's existing bill (cost avoidance) or new settled income (enrolled revenue) — that distinction is what drives which streams the 15% revenue share applies to.

4CP avoidance — cumulative value by forecast accuracy

The engine treats the 4 annual 4CP intervals as a step function, not a smooth discount — missing a call loses that interval's value entirely. Each line is one of the 5 discrete outcomes (0 of 4 through 4 of 4 correctly called), projected flat over a 5-year horizon at the current MW and rate. The line matching your current forecast accuracy slider is highlighted; the rest are shown for reference.

Market condition range — RT avoidance & net value

RT price spike avoidance is the one stream that swings with grid volatility. This holds MW and forecast accuracy at their current values and shows the annual RT avoidance and resulting net value across the weak / base / strong range. The current market condition is highlighted.
