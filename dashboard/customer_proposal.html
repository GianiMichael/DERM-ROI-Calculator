Understanding Your Value

This proposal combines two different kinds of value, and it's worth knowing the difference between them:

Lower bills (cost avoidance)

Some of this value never shows up as a payment to you — it shows up as a smaller number on your existing utility bill. You don't receive a check; you simply pay less than you would have.

New income (enrolled revenue)

The rest is new money that didn't exist before — payments for keeping your facility available to help the grid. This shows up as new income on top of your normal operations.
